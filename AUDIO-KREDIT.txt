PIKO AUDIO — ENGLISH EDITION

All active English guides and stories are prerecorded using Microsoft Zira Desktop (en-US), then encoded as MP3 files. The app plays the included files and needs no installed voice, API key, or internet connection after the offline download.

The earlier Indonesian edition used Jessica via ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io), Free plan, for noncommercial use. Those recordings are not part of this English edition.
